Conventions

UTM

Paid Google: utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign={_campaign}&utm_term={_adgroup}&utm_content={creative} (All-States uses static values and no utm_content: gap G1). Organic: utm_source=<network>&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=credo_nd_organic_v1&utm_term=<cluster>&utm_content=<post key>. Meta: utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid_social.

Phone numbers

One number per campaign × ad group × platform in content/campaigns/campaign-phone-numbers.csv; the page swaps its number by utm_source (shared code in Webflow site settings). New pages without an entry show the default (718) 865-8350.

Joins

Ad ↔ page on the page address (slug). Analytics ↔ ad on the ad id in utm_content (Google’s own creative id from the next refresh). Organic ↔ Analytics on utm_content. CRM ↔ ad through the Google Ads uploads (click id).

Snapshots

Reconciliation rule: ad figures must equal the account totals exactly; Analytics row sums may differ from the property total by up to 0.1% (the API omits rows below its reporting threshold). The 28-day snapshot sums to 7,569 sessions against 7,571 in the property.

Metrics tier

Spend, visits and client stages live in a separate file (data/metrics.js) that is never mirrored in plaintext. Planned: an encrypted sibling surface decrypted in the browser after one password entry per session (operator holds the password). Until then the hub is local only.
